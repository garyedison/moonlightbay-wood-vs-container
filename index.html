Rules for the review

Do not add Miguel’s $80/sf on top of a Plett finished house. Plett already billed cabinets, showers, paint-primer, spray foam, and delivery. Miguel’s rate is the Path 2 finish on a steel module.
Do not add the KDK four-worker fly-in (US$9,440) on top of Miguel Path 2. One crew.
Plett quotes expired on paper (valid 30 days from 27–28 July 2026). Ask Anne for a Consejo / Moonlight Bay reissue, in Belize dollars, with real transport to the lot. The 30×40 $0 freight/posts/windows is a promo.
CASA 100 installed figures ($64k–$89k) are a 100-home government campaign. They do not belong on a one-lot Moonlight Bay flyer unless that is stated.
